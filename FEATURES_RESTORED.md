# Included application features

This clean source tree uses the latest GenSolar Pro 1.4 Android package as its baseline. The welcome page, splash resources, logo assets, and existing calculator styles are retained.

## Existing user-facing areas retained
- Welcome, Login, Explore, Phone OTP, session restore, and app back navigation
- Solar estimator for residential, commercial, and industrial segments
- State / tariff inputs for Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, and Chhattisgarh
- Monthly bill or units inputs, roof area, usage offset, subsidy and accelerated-depreciation assumptions
- Yield, cost, grid rate, escalation, degradation, maintenance and inverter replacement assumptions
- Panel rating, estimated panel count, roof-area estimates, 25-year savings/payback analysis, quote sharing and PDF output
- Customer estimates, representative/manager lead workflow, follow-up notes, CSV sharing, profile and photo

## Added Engineering & Site Design workspace
- Site name, state, latitude and longitude
- Target DC capacity, panel technology, panel wattage, roof area and planning area per kW
- Array azimuth and tilt, planned inverter capacity, mounting type, and preliminary DC/AC ratio
- Fields for GHI, DNI, DHI, GTI, annual specific yield, CUF, and source/report date
- Estimated panel count, planning area, roof-limited capacity, and generation from an entered annual yield
- Device-saved pre-sales checklist: electricity bill, sanctioned load, roof photos, structural confirmation, layout/shadow notes, meter/DISCOM data, customer details and survey
- Basic project delivery stages from lead qualification through commissioning

## Important limitations
- Solar-resource figures are user-entered and must be sourced from a verified report or validated simulation. The app does not claim to fetch live satellite data.
- Engineering outputs are preliminary aids, not signed engineering design or a structural certification.
- Existing estimate/lead persistence behavior remains as implemented in the baseline; no new cloud-sync guarantee is introduced by this workspace.
